# Crew: short design

Asked for: every vehicle needs at least one crew member to work properly; the player alone can run the starting rig; more crew makes it work better; roles Captain, Driver, Mechanic, Gunner, Comms & Navigation, Medic; crew can be wounded or killed, especially under heavy damage; each has a name, personality and picture. The user said "just dive in", so the choices below are mine.

## Rules

- **Road Captain** is the player: always aboard the lead rig, never wounded, no bonus.
- **Seats:** lead rig 2 / 4 / 5 (scout, hauler, juggernaut), plus the captain. Escorts tier + 1.
- **Escorts need a fit crew member.** Without one they are towed: no guns, sweeping or scouting.
- **Bought escorts** come with one green hand (skill 0), who can run it but gives no bonus. Old saves get the same.
- **Bonuses per vehicle, best fit person of a role only:** driver (speed; on the lead also less fuel), mechanic (slow hull repair on the move, up to 60%), gunner (damage, accuracy), comms & navigation (sight and flee chance, best in the caravan), medic (heals crew and may save a dying one).
- **Wounds:** health 0-100; below 40 a person cannot work. Injuries come from battle damage, more likely the bigger the share of hull lost; a wrecked vehicle is very dangerous. Sun heat does not injure.
- **Healing:** slowly over time, faster with a medic, or paid treatment in a city.
- **Hiring:** each city offers four recruits, changing every 15 minutes. Skill 1-3. A reserve holds 3 people without a seat.
- **Personality:** one trait each; some change a rule (injury, healing, luck, price), others are colour only.
- **Pictures:** pixel portraits painted in code.
- **Screens:** CREW tab in cities to hire; C on the road (or from that tab) to see, move and dismiss crew.

## Not included

Wages, experience gain, relationships between crew, crew at baron mines.
