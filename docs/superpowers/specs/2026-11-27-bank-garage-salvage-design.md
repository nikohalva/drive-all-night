# Bank, garages and salvage: short design

Asked for: a garage in the starting city for spare parts and vehicles, with more to buy or rent in other cities; a bank shared by the cities (not the mines) so a lost fight does not take all your money; parts and maybe vehicles from fights.

## Choices made

- Losing a fight takes all carried credits. Without this the bank would protect nothing.
- Raiders answering a distress call still take only cargo.
- The rig has limited spare-part room, so storing matters. Older saves over the limit keep their parts.
- Rent buys 20 minutes of world time; contents are never lost.
- Captured vehicles are damaged and uncrewed. Salvage with no room is left behind.

## Not included

Adding the player's own mine to the bank network (comes with running a mine), interest, selling spare parts.
